01

Overview

LoopBit built Arrow Escape: Puzzle Game as a self-contained puzzle game: slide the arrows out of the grid, chase stars, streaks and badges — all without an account. This policy exists because Google Play requires one and because you deserve to know what a game touches, even a small one. Short version: almost nothing leaves your phone.

By installing or playing Arrow Escape, you're agreeing to the practices described below. If something here doesn't sit right with you, the fastest fix is also the most effective one — uninstall the app, which removes every trace of local data along with it (more on that in §4 and §7).

02

Information we collect

What you give us

Nothing. There's no registration, login, profile, in-app purchase, or contact form anywhere in the app, so we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, or payment details.

What the app stores automatically

To remember your progress between sessions, the app saves a small set of gameplay values on your device using Android's local SharedPreferences storage:

  • Level progress — which levels are unlocked, stars earned, moves used, lives lost
  • Coins, total score, hint usage, and daily/weekly challenge progress
  • Play streaks and the calendar of days you've played
  • Your chosen avatar, avatar frame, sound/music/vibration and light-or-dark theme settings

None of this is tied to your identity, and none of it is transmitted to LoopBit or anyone else — it's read and written locally, the same way the app remembers where you left off.

03

How we use information

Because nothing is collected off your device, "use" is limited to running the game itself: restoring your progress when you reopen the app, applying your saved settings, and calculating stats like your star count or unlocked badges. We don't build advertising profiles, we don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't sell or rent anything — there's no personal data on our end to sell.

04

Where your data lives

Everything listed in §2 stays in local storage on your device. LoopBit doesn't operate a server for this app, so there's nowhere for it to sync to even if we wanted it to. There's currently no cloud save or cross-device sync — progress made on one phone won't follow you to another.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data permanently. Since it never left your device, there's no separate copy anywhere for us to remove.

05

Third-party services

Google Play services is the one third party involved today — used only for the in-app update check and to hand off to the Play Store when you tap "Rate the App." That interaction is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy.

Arrow Escape does not currently include advertising or analytics SDKs of any kind.

If that changes

As the game grows, we may add advertising (for example, Google AdMob), analytics (for example, Firebase Analytics), or an optional cloud-save account. None of that is switched on today. If we do turn any of it on, we'll update this policy first, move the "Effective" date at the top of this page, and — wherever the law requires it — ask for your consent before the feature activates.

06

Children's privacy

Arrow Escape is a general-audience puzzle game and isn't directed specifically at children under 13. Since we don't collect personal information from anyone, of any age, there's nothing collected from children either. If you believe a child has somehow provided us personal information, contact us at the address in §10 and we'll look into it.

07

Your choices

  • Permissions — turn off network access or vibration anytime in your device's app settings. Disabling network access just means the app stops checking for updates and can't open outbound links.
  • In-app settings — sound, music, haptics and light/dark theme are all adjustable directly from the Settings screen.
  • Deleting your data — uninstall the app. Because everything lives in local storage, that single action removes it completely.
08

Security

Because gameplay data never leaves your device, it's protected by whatever passcode, biometric lock, or encryption your device already uses. We follow ordinary secure-development practice for everything we build, but no method of storage is ever 100% guaranteed — so your device's own security is, in effect, the app's security too.

09

Changes to this policy

We'll post updates on this page and refresh the "Effective" date at the top whenever something meaningful changes — most likely if we ever switch on advertising, analytics, or accounts (see §5). Continuing to use Arrow Escape after an update means you accept the revised policy.
